# Modul 06: Follow-up & Escalation Cadence

Modul ini mendefinisikan ritme *follow-up*, protokol 3-touch points, dan tangga eskalasi terstruktur untuk memastikan konfirmasi tepat waktu tanpa merusak hubungan kerja.

---

## 1. Ritme Standar Tindak Lanjut (Follow-up Intervals)

| Skenario | Interval Touch 1 | Interval Touch 2 | Interval Touch 3 (Eskalasi) |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Persetujuan Eksekutif / Urgent** | 24 - 48 jam kerja | 72 jam kerja (via CC lead) | 96 jam kerja + direct call/ping |
| **Klien / Penawaran Komersial** | 3 hari kerja | 7 hari kerja | 14 hari kerja (*Breakup email*) |
| **Vendor / Supplier Task** | 48 jam sebelum tenggat | Hari-H tenggat (09:00 WIB) | Hari-H + 24 jam (Eskalasi Procurement Lead) |
| **Internal Cross-Team Request** | 3 hari kerja | 5 hari kerja | 7 hari kerja (CC Department Head) |

---

## 2. Struktur 3-Touch Follow-up System

### Touch 1: Gentle Reminder (Re-anchor)
Memfokuskan kembali perhatian pada nilai utama dan tanggal target asli tanpa mengulang seluruh isi surat panjang:
> *"Subject: RE: [Action Required] Q4 Cloud Infrastructure Budget Sign-off - Due Nov 25*  
> *Hi Hendra,*  
> *Bringing this to the top of your inbox. We need your sign-off by Wednesday, November 25 at 17:00 WIB to lock in AWS enterprise reserved pricing for Q4.*  
> *Please let me know if you need any adjustments to the comparison table sent below."*

### Touch 2: Impact & Clarification (Consequence Highlighting)
Menjelaskan dampak operasional atau risiko penundaan jika persetujuan tidak didapat:
> *"Hi Hendra,*  
> *Following up on the AWS agreement below. Without sign-off by tomorrow 17:00 WIB, the reserved instance reservation will expire, resulting in a 22% higher on-demand compute cost for December.*  
> *I have CC'd Budi Santoso (VP Eng) to confirm technical readiness. Can we proceed with Option A?"*

### Touch 3: The Graceful Breakup / Formal Escalation
Memberikan kejelasan penutupan status agar tugas tidak menggantung:
> *"Hi Hendra,*  
> *Since we have not received confirmation for the Q4 contract, we have paused the reservation process to prevent unbudgeted commitments. We will revisit this initiative during the Q1 planning cycle unless you advise otherwise by Friday noon."*

---

## 3. Tangga Eskalasi Hierarkis (Escalation Protocol)

1. **Level 0 (Direct Assignee)**: Komunikasi 1-on-1 dengan PIC terkait.
2. **Level 1 (Visibility Add)**: Menambahkan manajer PIC ke baris `CC:` dengan kalimat transparan (*"Adding @[Manager Name] for visibility on project timeline"*).
3. **Level 2 (Executive Intervention)**: Email langsung ke pembuat keputusan utama dengan lampiran riwayat komunikasi dan ringkasan risiko terukur.
