# Modul 05: Tone, Diplomacy & Assertiveness

Modul ini menetapkan pedoman untuk kalibrasi nada komunikasi bisnis: asertif, profesional, tegas namun tetap santun (tactful diplomacy), tanpa meminta maaf secara berlebihan (*excessive apologizing*).

---

## 1. Spektrum Nada Komunikasi (Tone Matrix)

| Dimensi | Yang Dihindari (Passive / Weak) | Standar MailCraft (Assertive Diplomatic) | Yang Dihindari (Aggressive) |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Permintaan / Delegasi** | *"I was just wondering if maybe you had time to look at this?"* | *"Please review the attached contract by Friday, 15:00 WIB."* | *"You must review this now or else."* |
| **Keterlambatan Pihak Lain** | *"Sorry to bother you again, no rush though!"* | *"Following up on our milestone review. Please provide your input today so we stay on track."* | *"Why haven't you answered my email yet?"* |
| **Penolakan Permintaan** | *"I'm so sorry, I wish I could help but unfortunately I'm too busy."* | *"I am unable to take on this task due to committed Q3 deliverables. I recommend contacting [Name] for support."* | *"That's not my job."* |
| **Koreksi Kesalahan** | *"I could be wrong, but didn't we agree otherwise?"* | *"According to section 4.2 of our agreement, the revised scope requires an addendum."* | *"You clearly didn't read the agreement."* |

---

## 2. Aturan Eliminasi Apology Inflation

Hindari meminta maaf untuk situasi di mana tidak ada kelalaian atau kesalahan yang terjadi. Ubah permohonan maaf pasif menjadi apresiasi konstruktif:

- ❌ *"Sorry for the delay in getting back to you."*
  👉 ✅ *"Thank you for your patience."*
- ❌ *"Sorry to bother you again."*
  👉 ✅ *"Thank you for taking the time to address this priority item."*
- ❌ *"I'm sorry if this is confusing."*
  👉 ✅ *"Let me clarify the key objectives."*
- ❌ *"Sorry, but I don't think we can do that discount."*
  👉 ✅ *"Our pricing model reflects our dedicated engineering tier and is non-negotiable."*

---

## 3. Menegakkan Batasan Ruang Lingkup (Scope Defense)

Saat klien atau stakeholder meminta pekerjaan di luar perjanjian (scope creep), gunakan teknik *Positive No*:

1. **Afirmasi Nilai**: Akui relevansi ide tersebut bagi produk atau proyek.
2. **Definisi Batas Realistis**: Jelaskan batasan kontrak / timeline saat ini secara faktual.
3. **Solusi Alternatif**: Tawarkan *change order* atau *phase 2 inclusion*.

### Contoh Template:
> *"The requested analytics export module would be a valuable addition to the portal. Because our current Sprint 6 scope is dedicated to core payment integration to meet the November 30 go-live, we can incorporate this feature as Phase 2 scope or draft an addendum for an additional 2-week cycle. Let me know which approach aligns better with your launch strategy."*
